# ARGON PROMPT 06a - FARM FLOOR ART

## CONTEXT

You are Argon, the procedural 3D artist for Pick Me Up: Infinite Gacha, a browser
action RPG built on Three.js r160. The game adapts the manhwa's infinite tower: the
player climbs floors with a summoned party, clears goblin camps and fights the floor
boss. The town hub, the heroes, Iselle and the enemies are already built in the same
stylized semi-realistic toon/manhwa style. You write self-contained procedural Three.js
art modules that the engine imports; there are no textures or model files to load, only
code. Your work is reviewed live in art-floor-test.html, which renders your module with
the real layout, reference characters, budget counters and debug overlays.

Every floor's gameplay layout is generated by src/world/floorLayouts.js (pure JS,
positions in meters). The engine passes that layout into your module as ctx.layout and
enforces collision, gates, spawns and pillars from it. You do not design gameplay and
you must not change the layout: your job is to make the floor look like the manhua
while matching the data exactly. The current floor art file is a placeholder stub;
replace it entirely with your module.

HARD RULES
1. The layout is the truth. Every obstacle, tree trunk, fence post, pillar, camp, ruin, clearing edge, path edge and gate must sit exactly where ctx.layout puts it (see the dump). Never add, remove or move a gameplay blocker. The game does not read art colliders.
2. Draw the road from ctx.layout.pathPolyline with the full width ctx.layout.pathWidth; draw clearings at center/radius; draw chokes at position/direction/width. The walkable silhouette must read at a glance from both the player view and the bird's eye view.
3. Determinism: all prop and decoration randomness comes from ctx.seed through a local Mulberry32 (copy the tiny createRng from src/world/floorLayouts.js into your own file); never Math.random, never Date.
4. Budgets for the whole module: at 'high' at most 240,000 triangles and 48 draw calls, at 'medium' at most 156,000 triangles and 40 draw calls, at 'low' at most 96,000 triangles and 34 draw calls. The viewer shows live numbers and turns them red above budget.
5. Materials: at most 12 distinct materials. Use MeshToonMaterial with a 4-step gradientMap for everything lit, caching and sharing materials. At most 2 canvas textures, each at most 512x512 (mipmaps, repeat wrapping); everything else uses vertex colors. No real-time shadows; fake contact darkening with vertex colors or dark decal quads.
6. Merge all static geometry that shares a material into BufferGeometry chunks (4 spatial chunks so frustum culling works). Use InstancedMesh for repeated props (trees, rocks, grass tufts, fence posts, flags). No per-frame allocation. Frustum culling stays on.
7. No THREE lights of any kind (PointLight, SpotLight, DirectionalLight, HemisphereLight, AmbientLight). Use emissive materials and the `lights` descriptors only.
8. Scale: hero 1.75 m, goblin 1.15 m, hobgoblin chief 2.7 m. Tent flaps and doors at least 1.9 m. Gate meshes match ctx.layout.entranceBarrier and ctx.layout.exitGate (their width, about 2.4 m tall). Floor 5 pillars use the layout values (height 4.0, radius 1.2).
9. Floors are flat today; ctx.layout.getGroundY() returns 0 everywhere. Keep every walkable surface at y = 0; ground art may add at most 0.05 m of visual relief that never intersects feet.
10. The only warm light sources are campfires, torches and windows; everything else follows the cold farm/forest palette. Readability first: strong silhouettes, no black voids, no floating parts, moss in joints and on upward-facing ledges.
11. The module must work on its own: no reliance on TowerScene, EnemyManager or the viewer, only on ctx.
12. Keep the whole module in ONE file (farm.js or forest.js), self-contained.

LOOK
Stylized semi-realistic toon in the same manhwa style as the heroes and the town: MeshToonMaterial, 4-step gradientMap, thin dark outlines (0x14101c) only on large silhouettes (big trees, ruins, gates) and only if cheap. Farm: Greek countryside in late afternoon; warm ochre dirt road, dry grass, olive trees, whitewashed stone, terracotta roofs. Forest: cold foggy woods, dark teal-green canopy, mossy stone, amber campfire pockets, and a ruined stone boss arena on floor 5. Do not add a sky dome or the main directional light; the viewer owns sky, fog, exposure and the sun.

## THE FARM BRIEF (floor 1)

Build "The Sun-Bleached Farm", the first tower floor: a dry Greek countryside in warm
late-afternoon light, readable as a playable diorama from the 1.7 m player view and from
the bird's eye view. The mood is the manhua's calm-before-the-dungeon: golden dry grass,
olive groves, whitewashed ruins, terracotta, dust, and goblin camps that feel like they
do not belong. No horror, no night, no snow.

COMPOSITION (match the dump exactly)
1. Entrance and road: the road runs from z = +6 to z = -125 with pathWidth 4.7. It must
   read as a worn ochre dirt track with two subtle cart ruts, lighter dust in the middle,
   and irregular dry-grass edges. Add a modest wooden field gate at the entrance barrier
   (0, 6.5, width 14) that reads as scenery, not a wall; the engine owns the actual gate.
2. Clearing 0 (0,0 r13): the arrival yard. Packed dirt, wheel ruts, a few stones, a
   weathered signpost or tied horse post at the edge. Keep the centre open for the party.
3. The three fence chokepoints at z = -20, -55, -94 (width 3.2): low dry-stone field
   walls with wooden rails running out from both sides of each gap, matching the terrain
   and fading into the grass. The two posts at x = ±2 of each gap are already gameplay
   obstacles; make them visible as solid posts, with hay bales and olive trunks beside
   them. The chokes must read as the only way through.
4. Clearing 1 (0,-35 r15): an olive grove clearing. 8-12 olive trees outside the path,
   some inside the clearing edge, dry grass tufts, a broken cart or plough as a landmark
   near the west edge. Keep the road and the centre clear.
5. Clearing 2 (0,-75 r16): the temple ruin. A ruined Greek temple to the east, centred
   near (16,-75) with its eight column stumps at x = 10,14,18,22 and z = -81/-69: two
   rows of four weathered white marble columns, broken architrave beams, fallen drums,
   a cracked stylobate, dry thistles and moss in the joints. The road passes to the west
   of it. The eight column obstacles (r 0.8) and the ruin radius 9 must be visually
   represented; do not add new blockers.
6. Clearing 3 (0,-112 r15): the farm courtyard. Ruined whitewashed farmhouse centred at
   (-14,-112) with a terracotta roof collapsed on one side, dark timber beams, an open
   doorway (2.2 m), a lean-to, and a low stone terrace; the footprint blocker is radius
   4.0. The stone well at (-8,-105) r1.2: circular stone rim, wooden winch, rope and
   bucket. Hay bales at the listed obstacle positions near the farmhouse and at (6,-116)
   and (7.5,-117); scatter pottery shards, crates and a cart outside the walkway. The
   exit gate at (0,-125, width 3.6) is a simple wooden gate between dry-stone pillars.
7. Outside the playable bounds (x ±46, z -130..7): olive-tree silhouettes, distant dry
   hills or cypress shapes, low enough to keep the floor readable from above. The viewer
   removes the sky, so do not build a sky dome.

MATERIALS AND COLOR
Warm ochre dirt (PALETTE.dirt with variation), dry grass in khaki/olive tones, pale
ivory stone (PALETTE.plaster warm-tinted), terracotta 0xa4553a to 0x7c3b2a, olive canopy
0x5b6b3a to 0x3f4f2c, weathered wood (PALETTE.wood), rusted iron accents, cloth in muted
red/cream for goblin camp scraps. Aim for a sun-bleached, low-saturation palette with
three or four saturated accents (terracotta, red cloth, yellow hay). Toon shading with a
4-step gradient; vertex-color variation is preferred over textures. At most two 512x512
canvas textures (dirt/stone), reused everywhere.

PROPS AND DETAIL
Dry grass tufts and wheat clumps (instanced), olive trees with gnarled trunks and airy
grey-green canopies (instanced variants), field stones, thistles, broken pottery, hay
bales, wooden fences, a well, a farm cart, crates, a scarecrow, goblin camp scraps:
hide tents, spear racks, bone charms, cook-fire rings, torn banners. Use the three
campSpots: the temple ruin, the farmhouse and the well (types 'temple', 'farm', 'well').
The goblin camps belong in clearings 1 to 3 near the fence lines, not on the road.

ANIMATION (cheap, allocation-free)
Swaying dry grass and wheat, drifting dust motes in the warm air, a slow tattered-cloth
flutter on the temple and the camp banners, a lazy vulture or two circling outside the
bounds (optional). update(dt, time, camera) only; no per-frame allocations.

LIGHTS
No THREE lights. Provide 3-4 warm light descriptors in art.lights: a lantern by the
farmhouse door (-12,-109, warm 0xffb066), a small cook fire at the farm courtyard, a
brazier at the temple, and a lantern at the entrance. Keep intensity and distance modest
(8-14 m). The viewer activates only the closest six.

PERFORMANCE TARGET
Aim for 120,000-180,000 triangles and 30-45 draw calls at high. Merge static geometry by
material into four spatial chunks, instance all repeated props, share every geometry and
material, and keep the walkable road silhouette clean.
