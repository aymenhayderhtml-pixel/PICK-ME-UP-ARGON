# ARGON PROMPT 06c - PRAIRIE FLOOR ART

## CONTEXT

You are Argon, the procedural 3D artist for Pick Me Up: Infinite Gacha, a browser action RPG built on Three.js r160. The game adapts the manhwa's infinite tower: the player climbs floors with a summoned party, clears goblin and wolf encounters, and fights the floor boss. The town hub, the heroes, Iselle and the enemies are already built in the same stylized semi-realistic toon/manhwa style. You write self-contained procedural Three.js art modules that the engine imports; there are no textures or model files to load, only code. Your work is reviewed live in art-floor-test.html, which renders your module with the real layout, reference characters, budget counters and debug overlays.

Every floor's gameplay layout is generated by src/world/floorLayouts.js (pure JS, positions in meters). The engine passes that layout into your module as ctx.layout and enforces collision, gates, spawns and ambushes from it. You do not design gameplay and you must not change the layout: your job is to make the floor look like the manhwa while matching the data exactly. The current floor art file (src/art/floors/prairie.js) is a placeholder stub; replace it entirely with your module.

HARD RULES
1. The layout is the truth. Every obstacle, tree trunk, fence post, pillar, camp, ruin, clearing edge, path edge and gate must sit exactly where ctx.layout puts it (see the dump). Never add, remove or move a gameplay blocker. The game does not read art colliders.
2. Draw the road from ctx.layout.pathPolyline with the full width ctx.layout.pathWidth; draw clearings at center/radius; draw chokes at position/direction/width. The walkable silhouette must read at a glance from both the player view and the bird's eye view.
3. Determinism: all prop and decoration randomness comes from ctx.seed through a local Mulberry32 (copy the tiny createRng from src/world/floorLayouts.js into your own file); never Math.random, never Date.
4. Budgets for the whole module: at 'high' at most 240,000 triangles and 48 draw calls, at 'medium' at most 156,000 triangles and 40 draw calls, at 'low' at most 96,000 triangles and 34 draw calls. The viewer shows live numbers and turns them red above budget.
5. Materials: at most 12 distinct materials. Use MeshToonMaterial with a 4-step gradientMap for everything lit, caching and sharing materials. At most 2 canvas textures, each at most 512x512 (mipmaps, repeat wrapping); everything else uses vertex colors. No real-time shadows; fake contact darkening with vertex colors or dark decal quads.
6. Merge all static geometry that shares a material into BufferGeometry chunks (4 spatial chunks so frustum culling works). Use InstancedMesh for repeated props (trees, rocks, grass tufts, fence posts, flags). No per-frame allocation. Frustum culling stays on.
7. No THREE lights of any kind (PointLight, SpotLight, DirectionalLight, HemisphereLight, AmbientLight). Use emissive materials and the `lights` descriptors only.
8. Scale: hero 1.75 m, goblin 1.15 m, hobgoblin chief 2.7 m. Tent flaps and doors at least 1.9 m. Gate meshes match ctx.layout.entranceBarrier and ctx.layout.exitGate (their width, about 2.4 m tall). Floor 5 pillars use the layout values (height 4.0, radius 1.2).
9. Floors are flat today; ctx.layout.getGroundY() returns 0 everywhere. Keep every walkable surface at y = 0; ground art may add at most 0.05 m of visual relief that never intersects feet. The art terrain may continue and RISE outside the playable bounds (hills and mountain slopes) to hide the edge, as long as inside the bounds it equals getGroundY exactly.
10. The only warm light sources are campfires, torches and windows; everything else follows the cold farm/forest palette. Readability first: strong silhouettes, no black voids, no floating parts, moss in joints and on upward-facing ledges.
11. The module must work on its own: no reliance on TowerScene, EnemyManager or the viewer, only on ctx.
12. Keep the whole module in ONE file (farm.js, prairie.js or forest.js), self-contained.

LOOK
Stylized semi-realistic toon in the same manhwa style as the heroes and the town: MeshToonMaterial, 4-step gradientMap, thin dark outlines (0x14101c) only on large silhouettes (big trees, ruins, gates) and only if cheap. Prairie: open green prairie in cool overcast daylight; rolling grass hills on all sides, hazy blue-green mountains and dark forest tree line far away, packed pale earth dirt-and-gravel path with grass tufts, wide open meadow clearings, wind-swept long grass, wildflowers, scattered boulders, gray rock outcrops, a few lone windswept trees, wooden rail fences, low dry-stone walls, ancient stone ruins in meadow 2, and a trampled goblin campsite in meadow 3. Do not add a sky dome or the main directional light; the viewer owns sky, fog, exposure and the sun.

## PRAIRIE BRIEF

Floor 2 of the tower, an open green prairie in cool overcast daylight like the manhwa panels:
1. Environment & Atmosphere:
   - Rolling grass hills on all sides, hazy blue-green mountains and a dark forest tree line far away on the horizon.
   - The art terrain continues and RISES outside the playable bounds (hills and mountain slopes rising 4 to 18 m) to completely hide the outer edge, while keeping the ground inside bounds strictly at y = 0.
   - Cool overcast daylight mood: background 0x9fc3c0, fog 0xa9c9c4 (near 40, far 170), sun 0xeaf6ff from north-west.
2. Ground & Pathway:
   - A wide dirt-and-gravel path (width 5.8 m) of packed pale earth with grass tufts, matching `pathPolyline`.
   - Big open meadow clearings (`clearing_0` arrival yard, `clearing_1`, `clearing_2`, `clearing_3`).
   - Wind-swept long grass: instanced blades in 2 shades (`0x6f9a4a` and `0x86ad55`) with natural wave sway driven in `update(dt, time, camera)`.
   - Wildflower points in white (`0xf4f2ea`), pale yellow (`0xf5df6b`), and lilac (`0xb59bc7`).
3. Rock Formations & Obstacles:
   - Scattered boulders and gray rock outcrops placed precisely at the layout obstacle locations.
   - Ambush spots in tall grass and behind boulders matching `ctx.layout.ambushSpots` where prairie wolves wait.
4. Vegetation & Structures:
   - A few lone trees (windswept oaks and birches with stylized leafy canopies).
   - Wooden ranch-style rail fences and low dry-stone walls along sections of the path and choke points (at z = -20, -55, -94).
   - Ruined stone pillars and low ruined wall footings at `campSpots[0]` in meadow 2 (ruin type, r = 7.5).
   - Trampled goblin campsite at `campSpots[1]` in meadow 3 with fur bedrolls, crude fire pit with embers, and scattered bones.
   - Simple weathered timber post-and-lintel gate frames for entrance and exit.
5. Performance & Technical Limits:
   - Budget: at 'high' at most 240,000 triangles and 48 draw calls, at 'medium' at most 156,000 triangles and 40 draw calls, at 'low' at most 96,000 triangles and 34 draw calls.
   - At most 12 distinct materials. Caching and sharing materials.
   - At most 2 canvas textures (grass and stone, 512x512 with mipmaps and repeat wrapping).
   - All lit elements use MeshToonMaterial with 4-step gradientMap.
   - No THREE lights of any kind. Use emissive materials and the `lights` array descriptor.
