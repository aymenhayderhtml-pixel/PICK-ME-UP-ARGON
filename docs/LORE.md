# Pick Me Up: Infinite Gacha - Lore Reference

## 1. Premise & World
"Pick Me Up" is an infamous ultra-hardcore mobile gacha game notorious for its impossible difficulty and brutal permadeath mechanics: heroes who die in combat are permanently erased from the master's account.

The top player, "Loki" (ranked 5th globally, the only master capable of clearing extreme floors without excessive casualties), loses consciousness during a dungeon raid and awakens inside the game world not as a master, but as a weak **1-star warrior named Han Islat** in an obscure, struggling master account assigned to **Town #1377: Niflheimr**.

## 2. Niflheimr (The Hub)
A floating ancient stone plaza suspended in a star-speckled void.
- **Master**: The unseen player commanding from above, giving macro orders, summons, and resources.
- **Iselle**: The short-tempered, winged fairy administrator of Niflheimr who manages the town facilities, complains about the Master, and acts as the guide.
- **Key Facilities**:
  - **Summoning Altar**: The magic circle where the Master uses Gems and Summon Tickets to pull new heroes from other dimensions.
  - **Training Grounds**: Where heroes practice skills, gain experience, and refine their combat techniques.
  - **Synthesis Room**: Where heroes are merged, absorbed, or promoted to higher star ranks.
  - **Blacksmith**: Forging, repairing, and enhancing weapons and armor.
  - **Dormitory**: Where heroes rest, recover stamina/morale, and interact.
  - **Tower Gate**: The monolithic dimensional portal leading directly into the Tower of Mobius.

## 3. Core Cast
- **Han Islat**: Formerly the master Loki. Cold, analytical, experienced in all game mechanics, determined to reach Floor 100 to find answers. Starts with basic swordsmanship and shield techniques.
- **Jenna**: 1-star hunter/archer with a nervous demeanor but extraordinary situational awareness and the rare talent *Keen Sight*.
- **Aaron**: 1-star spearman who lacks self-confidence initially but possesses immense resilience and loyalty.
- **Velkist**: 1-star berserker with untamed combat instincts.
- **Eolka**: 4-star fire element mage, proud and fiery-tempered.
- **Sirris**: 4-star valkyrie with high combat prowess.

## 4. The Tower & Rules
- **Floors 1-100**: Each floor contains hostile dungeons, traps, and monsters. Bosses reside on milestone floors (e.g. Floor 5 Hobgoblin, Floor 10, etc.).
- **Squad System**: Up to 5 heroes form a raid squad, led in the field by Han with AI squad companions.
- **Permadeath**: Zero HP means permanent deletion. Heroes are not code to each other—they are living souls fighting for survival.
